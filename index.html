Pad / Pond Avg. Depth (Multi-Point)

For house pads, ponds & arenas with uneven grade — take a cut/fill reading at each grid point (corners + center is the standard 5-point method), and this averages them across the footprint. Cut = positive, fill = negative.

Enter area & at least one reading

Trench / Linear Cross-Section

Average end area method — good for trenches, swales & roads. Positive depth = fill (above grade). Negative depth = cut (below grade).

Trench floor width (cut) or fill crown width (fill) — the narrow end.

Typical: 1.5:1 to 2:1 for loose soil, 1:1 for stable clay.
